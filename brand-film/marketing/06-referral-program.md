# Firststone: Referral and Reviews Program v1

**Prepared with:** `referrals` skill (marketingskills), including the review-programs guidance
**Reads first:** `../.agents/product-marketing.md`, `identity.md` §08 (referral engine)
**Why it matters:** for an affordable local service, a neighbour's recommendation and a visible, well-made menu next door are the cheapest acquisition Firststone will ever have. The identity already designs for it (window sticker, "Made by Firststone" credit). This turns that into a program.

---

## 1. Viral potential: natural, through exposure

Firststone's output is customer-facing. Every delivered menu, screen and ad is seen by diners and by neighbouring owners. That puts it at the "natural" end of the skill's viral-potential spectrum, so the program leads with **product-embedded exposure**, and adds a small reward on top.

| Mechanism | What it is | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Credit line** | "Made by Firststone" in small mono type on screen footers and the bottom of printed menus | Default on; client can say no. Never on the client's own brand area |
| **Window sticker** | «متجهّز مع Firststone» ("Set up with Firststone"), mark on stone, with a QR to the WhatsApp chat | Ask at go-live; install only with permission. Print only after the name is cleared |
| **Before/after posts** | Client's old and new menu side by side on Firststone's Instagram | Only with written permission (founding clients agree to this) |

The sticker's QR opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message: «شفت الستيكر عند [المكان]. عايز أعرف أكتر.» That gives attribution for free: the referring shop's name arrives in the first message. Use one QR per shop (a `wa.me` link with the shop name in the text) so each sticker is traceable.

---

## 2. Owner-to-owner referral reward

**Trigger moments** (when to ask): go-live day, the first monthly "what changed" message, and right after a quick fix that went well.

**Reward (double-sided, paid in service, not cash):**
- **Referrer:** one free month of their plan when the referred shop goes live.
- **New client:** a seasonal menu (e.g., Ramadan) included free with their Menu setup.

Why service, not cash: it costs delivery hours rather than money, keeps the referrer on the plan (retention), and avoids "I'm being paid to recommend" awkwardness between neighbours. Present it as the bigger-feeling item: "a free month" reads better than a percentage.

**The ask (WhatsApp, after a good moment):**
> «مبسوطين إن المنيو الجديد شغال معاك. لو تعرف حد عنده كافيه أو محل ومحتاج نفس الحاجة، ابعتله رقمنا. لما مكانه يبقى جاهز، الشهر الجاي في باقتك علينا.»
> ("Glad the new menu is working for you. If you know someone with a café or shop who needs the same, send them our number. When their place goes live, next month of your plan is on us.")

**Rules:** one reward per referred shop that goes live and pays its setup; no reward for referring an existing lead; no cap for now (review at 20 clients).

## 3. Google reviews

**Policy:** never offer anything (discount, free month, gift) in exchange for a review, and never ask only happy clients to review. Google's policies prohibit incentivized and selectively solicited reviews, and a removed profile costs more than the reviews were worth.

**When to ask:** after the first monthly cycle, when the client has seen at least one update land correctly. Not on go-live day; it's too early to judge.

**Ask (`/review` quick reply):**
> «لو عندك دقيقة، رأيك على جوجل بيفرق معانا جداً وبيساعد محلات تانية تعرفنا: [رابط التقييم]. ولو في حاجة مش عاجباك، قولّنا هنا الأول نصلّحها.»
> ("If you have a minute, your opinion on Google really helps us and helps other shops find us: [review link]. And if something isn't right, tell us here first so we can fix it.")

**Replying to reviews:** every review gets a reply within 2 working days, in the reviewer's language. Negative review: thank, state what happened, say what was fixed, offer a direct line. No arguing in public.

---

## 4. Partner referrals

**Partners:** tablet and screen sellers, POS resellers, print shops in the chosen neighbourhoods.

**Offer:** a referral fee per partner-referred client who completes setup **[NEED: amount, or reciprocal referrals instead of cash]**, and a promise never to sell hardware or POS against them.

**Pitch line** (`messaging.md` §05): "We set up the look and the simple tools around your products." Start with 3 conversations in weeks 5–8; formalize with a one-page agreement only after the first partner-referred client.

## 5. Tracking (a sheet is enough)

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| New client | WhatsApp label `won` |
| How they heard | First message (sticker QR pre-fill, partner name, "my neighbour") or ask at the visit |
| Referrer (shop or partner) | Same |
| Reward owed / given | Updated at go-live |

**Metrics:** share of new clients from referral (target: a third by month 6, `01-marketing-plan.md` §1); stickers installed; reviews and average rating; plan retention of referred vs non-referred clients (the skill's benchmarks suggest referred customers stay longer; check whether that holds here).
