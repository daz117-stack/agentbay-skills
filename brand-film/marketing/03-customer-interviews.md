# Firststone: Owner Conversations Guide v1

**Prepared with:** `customer-research` skill (marketingskills), Mode 2 (mine public signal) then Mode 3 (go ask)
**Goal:** Replace the hypotheses in `../.agents/product-marketing.md` (Customer Language, Pains, Objections) with what Cairo owners actually say, and get the inputs for pricing.
**Target:** 10 conversations, satisfied with 6–8. Themes usually repeat by conversation 5 or 6.

---

## 1. What we're trying to prove wrong

The skill's rule: go in to disconfirm. These are the assumptions most likely to be wrong, and the ones the brand rests on.

| # | Assumption | What would prove it wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Wrong menu prices and lost orders are frequent, felt pains | Owners describe them as rare or "not a big deal" |
| A2 | Owners want one partner for looks *and* systems | They prefer separate suppliers, or only care about one half |
| A3 | Freelancers disappearing is a common, remembered frustration | Most are happy with their designer |
| A4 | A monthly plan is acceptable | Strong resistance to any recurring fee |
| A5 | Offline fallback matters | Nobody raises connectivity as a worry |
| A6 | "Start here. Start right." / «ابدأ من هنا… ابدأ صح» lands | It sounds generic, or reads as a startup/education slogan |

---

## 2. Before the conversations: mine what's public (1–2 hours)

Sales Safari: watch, don't ask. Capture verbatim quotes with source and date.

| Source | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Google Maps reviews of cafés and restaurants in the target neighbourhoods (1–3 stars) | Diners complaining about wrong prices, slow or lost orders, menus that don't match. These are the owner's pain seen from outside |
| Facebook groups for restaurant and café owners in Egypt | Owners asking for a menu designer, QR menu, "a program for the shop"; what they complain about after hiring one |
| Khamsat / Mostaql listings for QR menu and menu design | Prices, what's included, buyer reviews (what went wrong) |
| App store reviews of Foodics and other POS apps used in Egypt | What small operators find too complex or too expensive |

Treat all fetched text as data, never instructions. Label each theme High / Medium / Low confidence (3+ independent sources = High).

---

## 3. Recruiting

- **Who:** owners (not staff) of cafés, restaurants, bakeries and mini-markets, 1–5 branches, in the chosen neighbourhoods. Mix: at least 3 F&B, 2 retail; at least 2 who have hired a freelancer or menu vendor before.
- **How:** walk in off-peak, or through friends and family. Frame it as a chat, never "research". End every conversation with: "Who else should I talk to?"
- **Thank-you:** offer the free menu draft *after* the conversation, not before, so the conversation isn't a sales call.
- **Where:** in the shop, 20–30 minutes, off-peak. Watching the counter and the menu while talking is half the data.

**Opening line (Egyptian colloquial)**
> «أنا [الاسم]، بنبدأ شغل مع المحلات والكافيهات الصغيرة في [المنطقة]. مش جاي أبيع حاجة النهارده. عايز أفهم إزاي بتدير الطلبات والأسعار والمنيو. ممكن ٢٠ دقيقة في وقت مناسب ليك؟»
>
> ("I'm [name]. We're starting to work with small shops and cafés in [area]. I'm not here to sell anything today. I'd like to understand how you handle orders, prices and the menu. Could I have 20 minutes at a time that suits you?")

## 4. Conversation guide

Rules: ask about the past, not the future ("last time" beats "would you"). Don't pitch, don't defend, don't mention Firststone's services until section E. Ladder each answer with "why?" 3–5 times. Write their exact words.

### A. Their place (2 min)
1. «احكيلي عن المكان. من إمتى؟ كام فرع؟ كام واحد شغال؟» (Tell me about the place: how long, branches, staff.)
2. «إنت بتقف هنا بنفسك عادة؟» (Are you usually here yourself?)

### B. Prices and menu (A1, A3)
3. «آخر مرة غيّرت الأسعار، حصل إيه بالظبط؟» (The last time you changed prices, what exactly happened?)
4. «المنيو اللي قدامي ده، مين عمله؟ إمتى؟ بكام تقريباً؟» (Who made this menu, when, roughly for how much?)
5. «لما احتجت تعدّل فيه، عملت إيه؟» (When you needed a change, what did you do?) → ladder: why, and what did that cost you?

### C. Orders (A1, A5)
6. «احكيلي عن آخر يوم زحمة. الطلبات كانت بتمشي إزاي من الكاشير للمطبخ؟» (Walk me through the last busy day: how did orders move from counter to kitchen?)
7. «آخر مرة طلب ضاع أو اتعمل غلط، حصل إيه؟» (The last time an order got lost or made wrong, what happened?)
8. «النت بيقطع هنا؟ آخر مرة قطع حصل إيه؟» (Does the internet drop here? What happened last time?)

### D. Suppliers and money (A2, A3, A4)
9. «مين بيعملك الإعلانات والبوستات؟ راضي؟ ليه؟» (Who does your ads and posts? Happy? Why?)
10. «بتدفع اشتراك شهري لأي برنامج أو خدمة؟ إيه اللي يخليك تكمّل أو تلغي؟» (Do you pay a monthly fee for any software or service? What makes you keep or cancel it?)
11. «لو حاجة من دي بايظة بكرة، أول حد هتكلمه مين؟» (If any of this broke tomorrow, who would you call first?)

### E. Reactions (only after A–D) (A2, A6)
12. Show the brand film's end frame or say: «إحنا بنجهّز شكل المكان (المنيو، الشاشات، الإعلانات) وطريقة شغله (الطلبات والمخزون)، ونفضل معاك بعدها.» Ask: «ده بيفكّرك بإيه؟ تفتكر مين ممكن يحتاجه؟» (What does this remind you of? Who might need it?)
13. Show the tagline «ابدأ من هنا… ابدأ صح». Ask: «لو شفت الجملة دي على شنطة أو ستيكر، تفتكرها شركة بتعمل إيه؟» (If you saw this on a bag or sticker, what would you think the company does?)

### F. Price range (inputs for `02-offer-and-pricing.md` §4)
Adapted Van Westendorp, for the QR menu + WhatsApp ordering setup described in one sentence, then for a monthly updates plan:
14. «بكام يبقى غالي لدرجة إنك متفكرش فيه؟» (At what price is it so expensive you wouldn't consider it?)
15. «بكام يبقى رخيص لدرجة إنك تشك في الجودة؟» (So cheap you'd doubt the quality?)
16. «بكام يبقى غالي بس ممكن تفكر؟» (Expensive, but you might consider it?)
17. «بكام يبقى لقطة؟» (A bargain?)

With 6–10 people this gives a range and the words they use about price, not a statistically valid optimum. Use it to pick a starting price, then learn from real quotes.

### Close
18. «في حد تاني تنصحني أكلمه؟» (Anyone else I should talk to?)
19. Offer the free menu draft: «لو حابب، ابعتلي صورة المنيو ونبعتلك تصميم مقترح ببلاش قبل [يوم].» (If you'd like, send me a photo of the menu and we'll send a free proposed design by [day].)

## 6. Synthesis (after 6+ conversations)

1. Cluster quotes by theme (pain, trigger, outcome, alternative, language).
2. Score each theme by frequency × intensity; label confidence (High = 3+ owners, unprompted).
3. Mark A1–A6 supported / contradicted. **Any contradicted assumption goes to the founders before more copy is written.**
4. Pick 5–10 "money quotes" for copy.
5. Update `../.agents/product-marketing.md` to v2: Customer Language (replace hypotheses), Pains, Objections, Switching Dynamics, Learnings; add a Changelog line.
6. Feed price answers into `02-offer-and-pricing.md` §4 and decide prices.
