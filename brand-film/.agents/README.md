## Decisions and actions before launch

1. **Clear the name.** Trademark (Egypt ITDA plus target Gulf markets, Classes 9 / 35 / 42), domains (.com .eg .sa .ae), social handles. Fallbacks if it fails: Cornerline, Mainstay (see `naming.md`).
2. **Arabic logotype.** Transliteration «فيرست ستون» (recommended) vs. a translated name.
3. **Make the proofs true** before claiming them: package prices, support response time, go-live checklist, offline fallback per product (`positioning.md` §05).
4. **Founder story facts.** The origin moment and Hamdy's background (`story.md`).
5. **Employer policy.** Check outside-business and public-communication rules before naming any founder's employer or banking background publicly.
6. **Regulatory.** Verify Egyptian e-invoicing / e-receipt obligations before selling order or POS software.
7. **Validate with customers.** 5–10 owner conversations to confirm the audience language and the tagline (`brand-context.md` marks these as hypotheses).
