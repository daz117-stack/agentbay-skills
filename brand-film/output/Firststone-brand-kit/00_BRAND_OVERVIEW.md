## 2. What we do and for whom

- **One-liner:** We give everyday businesses the look and the simple systems of a well-run company (digital menus, ads, order and stock tools that just work) at prices small businesses can afford.
- **Services:**
  - Media and advertising: visuals, promotional content, digital menus, product ads
  - Simple business software
  - Restaurant and retail systems: orders across stations, stock
  - Small custom tools
- **Audience:** Owners of independent cafés, restaurants, bakeries, supermarkets and shops with 1–5 branches. Hands-on, price-aware, Arabic-first.
- **Their problem:** The business runs on paper slips, WhatsApp and outdated prices, and its visuals look improvised. Freelancers are cheap but disappear; platforms are capable but rigid and priced for chains.
- **Market position:** Affordable / volume pricing with premium-feeling execution.

## 3. Positioning

- **Category:** *the setup partner*. We set up how a business **looks** and how it **runs**, then stay.
- **In one sentence:** Firststone is the affordable setup partner that gets small cafés, restaurants and shops looking right and running right, and stays to keep them that way.
- **Public version:** "You run the place. We set up how it looks and how it works, and we stay to keep it that way."
- **Competitor map** ⚠ desk research:
  - Freelancers (Khamsat, Mostaql): one-off, unreliable.
  - Small agencies and print shops: visuals only.
  - QR-menu vendors (eMenu, QRMenuPro, Billqode): narrow.
  - Foodics, BIM POS / Compuware: systems only, built for chains.
  - elmenus: consumer discovery.
  - **Gap:** an ongoing partner for looks and systems, at small-business prices.
- **Moat:** the combination of design, systems and support from one team. It is thin in year one, so **retention is the strategy**.
- **We refuse to be:** the cheapest, an enterprise-software seller, a growth-hacking agency, or a vendor that locks clients in.

## 4. Messaging

- **Core:** Firststone helps small cafés, restaurants and shops start right and run smoothly, by setting up how they look and how they work, and staying to keep it that way.
- **Headline:** Start here. Start right.
- **Supporting line:** We set up how your business looks and how it runs (menus, ads, orders and stock), then stay to keep it working. One team, clear prices, no surprises.
- **Key messages:**
  1. One team for your look and your systems
  2. Built to run every single day
  3. Clear prices. Clear status. No surprises.
  4. Sized for small businesses
- **Proofs to make true before claiming them** ⚠:
  - published package prices
  - a written support response time (e.g. same business day on WhatsApp)
  - a go-live checklist and an offline fallback for each system
  - documented client cases (with permission)
- **Never say:** "best in Egypt", "guaranteed sales", "100% uptime", "digital transformation", "one-stop shop", "cheapest". No "POS/ERP" in headlines. Not "Start IT" (ITIDA's startup programme).

## 5. Voice

- **Essence:** a calm, capable neighbour who sets things up properly: clear, warm, and exact.
- **Qualities:** plain · calm · exact · warm · honest · practical.
- **Registers:**
  - Egyptian colloquial Arabic for social, WhatsApp and sales
  - Light formal Arabic (MSA) for contracts and Gulf audiences
  - English for the website and decks
- **Rules:** short sentences; numerals for prices and times; no exclamation marks in product, support or billing; at most one emoji; sentence case; active voice.
- **Use:** set up · start right · simple · clear · tested · handed over · «ابدأ صح» · «جاهز» · «مكانك»
- **Avoid:** solutions · cutting-edge · seamless · robust · world-class · «نحيطكم علماً» (stiff formal Arabic)

## 6. Visual identity

**Mark.** Two identical L-strokes (ink + cobalt) interlocking into a square with two open joints: the first stone, made of two equal parts (two founders, one structure).
Geometry: 200-unit box, 34-unit stroke, 44-unit gaps (`logo/*.svg`).

**Wordmark.** "Firststone" in Archivo ExtraBold, ~108% width, sentence case, custom kerning for "stst". No "FS" monogram.

**Palette (cobalt on stone):**

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cobalt (accent) | `#2F54EB` | Mark, highlights, offers, tagline. ≤5% of a layout. |
| Text on cobalt | `#FAF9F6` | ≈6:1 contrast. Never ink on cobalt (≈3:1). |
| Ink | `#121417` | Text, mark, dark plates |
| Stone | `#EEECE7` | Default background |
| Stone High | `#FAF9F6` | Cards, UI surfaces |
| Graphite | `#575B63` | Secondary text |
| Rule | `#D6D3CC` | Grids, dividers |
| Screen | `#151820` | Dark digital menus |

**Typography:**
- Display: Archivo ExtraBold–Black, width 108–125%
- UI and body: Inter
- Labels and data: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, tracked
- Arabic ⚠ to test: Alexandria or Readex Pro (display), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (body)

**Design principles:**
- **Built on the line:** 12-column grid; alignment is the decoration.
- **One signal:** cobalt marks the single thing that matters.
- **Real over polished:** real shops, real screens.
- **Legible in both languages.**

**Imagery:**
- Use: documentary, warm daylight, real counters, staff and screens, plus architectural line drawings.
- Avoid: stock handshakes, holograms, neon "tech", AI-generated shops, unpermitted client imagery.

**Touchpoints:**
- Same avatar (mark on stone) across all socials.
- Website hero "Start here. Start right." with a live demo and clear prices.
- Bilingual business cards.
- A "Made by Firststone" credit on delivered screens.
- Window sticker «متجهّز مع Firststone» ("Set up with Firststone") as the referral engine.
